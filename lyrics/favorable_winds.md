<b>Song for Favorable Winds</b><br/>
<br/>
Today, you embark on a long voyage<br/>
Do you have with you the soil and water from home?<br/>
May you never forget homeland and remember where you’re from<br/>
May you the wind fill your sails to safely cross the ocean<br/>
<br/>
Today, you depart<br/>
Have you eaten the sweet egg from home?<br/>
May your misfortunes turn to luck, sweet and full<br/>
May you prosper and flourish, returning home in silken robes<br/>
<br/>
In the homeland, the people are many and the fields barren<br/>
Across the South Sea, opportunities abound and the ocean is vast<br/>
Build a boat and head to sea, with blood we trade for<br/>
Six to die, three to live, one to return home<br/>
<br/>
Sending you to sea<br/>
Hoping you return home<br/>
Wishing you health<br/>
