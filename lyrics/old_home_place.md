<b>老家鄉</b><br/><br/>
我離開老家已經十來年<br/>
我生長的小小村庄<br/>
秋夜颼颼涼、炊煙裊裊升<br/>
蟋蟀叫聲輕輕響<br/>
<br/>
我愛上城鎮的小姑娘<br/>
以為他真心始終<br/>
我離開家跑到邵武去<br/>
在木材場辛勤打工<br/>
<br/>
哦怎麼辦我的老家鄉<br/>
為什麼全部都被拆<br/>
為什麼我不在家乡种田<br/>
而去大城當打工仔<br/>
<br/>
哦那姑娘他突然跟別人跑掉<br/>
我喝醉，錢也都花光<br/>
現在我回到曾經的家<br/>
眼前只有空蕩蕩<br/>
<br/>
寒風吹，大雁往南飞<br/>
我站這兒垂頭喪氣<br/>
我失了戀，又沒了家<br/>
我不如现在死去<br/>

哦怎麼辦我的老家鄉<br/>
為什麼全部都被拆<br/>
為什麼我不在家乡种田<br/>
而去大城當打工仔<br/>

<b>Old Home Place</b><br/><br/>
It’s been ten long years since I left my home<br/>
In the hollow where I was born<br/>
Where the cool fall nights make the wood smoke rise<br/>
And the foxhunter blows his horn<br/>
<br/>
I fell in love with a girl from the town<br/>
I thought that she would be true<br/>
I ran away to Charlottesville<br/>
And worked in a sawmill or two<br/>
<br/>
What have they done with the old home place<br/>
why did they ever tear her down?<br/>
why did I leave my plow in the field<br/>
to look for a job in the town<br/>
<br/>
Well my girl she ran off with somebody else<br/>
The taverns took all my pay<br/>
And here I stand where the old home stood<br/>
Before they took it away<br/>
<br/>
Now the geese they fly south and the cold wind blows<br/>
As I stand here and hang my head<br/>
I’ve lost my love I’ve lost my home<br/>
And now I wish that I was dead<br/>
